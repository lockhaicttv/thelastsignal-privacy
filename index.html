Privacy Policy for The Last Signal

Last Updated: April 17, 2026

Quick Summary: The Last Signal is a social deduction game that respects your privacy. We don't collect personal information, and all your game data stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Custom Keywords: If you create custom keyword pairs, they are stored locally on your device only.
  • Game Settings: Your preferences and game configurations are stored locally.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Purchase History: When you make in-app purchases through Google Play, the transaction is processed by Google Play Billing. We store a local record of your purchases to unlock content.
  • Game Progress: Your unlocked keyword packs and game statistics are stored locally on your device.

1.3 Information We DO NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, or contact information
  • We do not collect your location data
  • We do not track your browsing or usage patterns
  • We do not collect device identifiers for advertising purposes
  • We do not use cookies or tracking technologies

2. How We Use Information

The information stored locally on your device is used solely to:

  • Provide game functionality and features
  • Remember your unlocked keyword packs
  • Save your custom keywords and game settings
  • Restore your purchases if you reinstall the app

3. Data Storage

  • Local Storage: All game data is stored locally on your device using SQLite database.
  • No Cloud Sync: We do not upload or sync your data to any remote servers.
  • Data Control: You have full control over your data. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

4. In-App Purchases

  • All purchases are processed through Google Play Billing
  • We do not collect or store your payment information
  • Purchase transactions are subject to Google's Privacy Policy
  • We store a local record of your purchases to unlock premium content
  • You can restore purchases at any time through the app's Store screen

5. Third-Party Services

The Last Signal uses the following third-party services:

5.1 Google Play Services

  • Purpose: App distribution and in-app purchases
  • Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5.2 Expo Framework

  • Purpose: App development framework
  • Privacy Policy: https://expo.dev/privacy

6. Children's Privacy

The Last Signal is intended for users aged 13 years and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

7. Data Security

  • All data is stored locally on your device and protected by your device's security measures
  • We do not transmit your personal data over the internet
  • Purchase transactions are secured by Google Play's infrastructure

8. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access: View all data stored in the app through the game interface
  • Delete: Remove all data by uninstalling the app or clearing app data in device settings
  • Restore Purchases: Use the "Restore Purchases" button in the Store screen to recover your purchases

9. Data Retention

  • Data is retained locally on your device until you uninstall the app or clear app data
  • Purchase records with Google Play are retained according to Google's policies

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy on this page
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Showing an in-app notification (for significant changes)

Your continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

11. International Users

The Last Signal is available globally. Your data is stored locally on your device in your country. We do not transfer data across borders.

12. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect (see Section 1)
  • Request deletion of your information (uninstall the app)
  • Opt-out of sale of personal information (we do not sell your information)

13. GDPR Compliance (European Users)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the right to:

  • Access: Request access to your personal data
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data (uninstall the app)
  • Data Portability: Export your custom keywords (via Excel export feature)
